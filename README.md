📌 Why Flat Measure vs. Body Anatomy Matters
In premium women's activewear manufacturing, high-density fabric elastane ratios, high-compression hold, and zero-transparency performance require precise negative ease measurement adjustments.

Flat Measure: The dimensions of the fitness leggings when laid completely flat on an inspection table.

Body Anatomy: The actual physical measurements of the athlete's body.

The Formula: Engineering engineered negative ease based on high-gauge Polyamide, Spandex, and custom double-knit interlock blends to deliver maximum muscular support, full opacity, and an uncompromised 100% squat-proof fit.
