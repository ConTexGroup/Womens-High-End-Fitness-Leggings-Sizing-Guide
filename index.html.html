💡 Key Engineering Rule for Activewear Designers & Brand Owners:
A garment cut from rigid woven fabric (such as tailored trousers) requires Positive Ease (+4 cm to +8 cm larger than the body) to allow movement. Women's high-end fitness leggings require Negative Ease (-8% to -18%) so the 4-way stretch elastane fibers engage immediately upon dressing—delivering core tummy control, glute-lifting contour, and zero waistband roll-down during high-impact training.

🔬 The Science: Why High-End Fitness Leggings Cannot Be Cut 1:1 to Body Size

❌ Case A: Cutting 1:1 (71.5 cm Table Measure)

Result: Critical Performance Failure During Workouts.
If the high-rise waistband is cut flat at 35.75 cm (71.5 cm loop) to match the athlete's exact waist circumference:

  • Zero modulus tension to anchor the high waistband during running, box jumps, or burpees.
  • The leggings constantly slide down during deep squats and deadlifts, forcing the athlete to pull them up mid-set.
  • Unstretched fabric pools and bunches at the front groin, hip flexors, and behind the knees.
  • Zero glute-sculpting lift or muscle-stabilizing compression, resulting in a baggy, low-end appearance.

✅ Case B: Conbello Engineered Fit (33.0 cm Table Measure)

Result: Zero-Defect Luxury Activewear Performance.
With our engineered -5.5 cm negative ease (33.0 cm flat 1/2 waist):

  • The 22% Lycra® Spandex core activates 360° graduated compression for muscle support and blood flow.
  • Bonded high-rise tummy-control waistband locks smoothly above the hip bones without rolling down or digging in.
  • High-gauge interlock knit remains 100% squat-proof (zero transparency under full glute flexion).
  • Diamond crotch gusset and flatlock sculpting seams contour the body cleanly with zero chafing.
